# CCBC Canva Build Brief

## GOAL

- Create the official CCBC institutional brand guide.
- Primary audience: current CCBC members and communicators.
- Secondary audience: young families in Candelaria.
- Success: welcoming, biblical, family-oriented, Clear Contemporary, easy to print and maintain.
- Source of truth for content: `brand-guide/content/ccbc-brand-guide-copy.md`.
- Render the tagline exactly: **Going for Christ, Growing in Christ**.

## FORMAT

- A4 portrait, 210 × 297 mm.
- 18 pages.
- 15 mm outer safe margin.
- 18 mm binding-side safe margin.
- 3 mm bleed for any full-bleed professional-print page.
- Design for both screen reading and ordinary A4 office printing.
- Do not place required content in the bleed or non-printable edge area.

## LAYOUT

- Use a disciplined six-column grid with 4 mm gutters.
- Use an 8 pt baseline rhythm.
- Prefer large, left-aligned headings.
- Give each page one dominant message.
- Use generous negative space and asymmetrical but balanced compositions.
- Use large blue fields for identity, Clear Sky shapes for energy, and Gold only for focused accents.
- Use restrained angled bands, rising lines, and expanding blocks to suggest forward movement and growth.
- Keep functional details together in one scannable block.
- Maintain consistent folios and section labels.
- Use three reusable patterns:
  1. Statement page — one dominant heading, short support copy, large color field.
  2. Standards page — heading, explanation, labeled examples, compact rule block.
  3. Application page — realistic mockup at dominant scale, concise annotation column.

## TYPE SYSTEM

- Manrope 800 or 700 for display headings.
- Manrope 600 for subheadings.
- Inter 400 for body copy.
- Inter 600 for labels, metadata, and functional emphasis.
- Arial only as a fallback when brand fonts are unavailable.
- Print body copy: minimum 10.5 pt.
- Print captions and folios: minimum 8.5 pt.
- Heading leading: tight but never colliding.
- Body leading: comfortable and readable.
- Micro labels: uppercase only when short, with modestly expanded tracking.
- Prefer sentence case elsewhere.
- Keep paragraphs left-aligned.
- Establish hierarchy through size, weight, and spacing rather than extra typefaces.

## COLOR + MATERIAL

- **CCBC Blue — #1E3A8A:** primary brand fields, covers, headers, and emphasis.
- **Deep Navy — #172554:** text, overlays, formal applications, and strong contrast.
- **CCBC Gold — #C8A64B:** rules, highlights, icons, and restrained accents.
- **Clear Sky — #6DB7DD:** fresh supporting panels, shapes, and event graphics.
- **Warm Cream — #FAF8F3:** preferred warm light background.
- **Charcoal — #1F2937:** primary body text.
- **White — #FFFFFF:** clarity, contrast, and low-ink layouts.

Target overall balance:

- 60% Warm Cream or White
- 25% CCBC Blue or Deep Navy
- 10% Clear Sky
- 5% CCBC Gold

Use flat, print-reliable color. Avoid glossy effects, heavy texture, and ornamental treatment. Gold and Clear Sky must not be used for small text on White.

## IMAGERY / UI STYLE

- Style lane: Clear Contemporary.
- Use authentic, people-centered, multigenerational church imagery from CCBC and Candelaria.
- Target 60% candid photography and 40% polished photography.
- Candid images: worship, fellowship, service, discipleship, and everyday ministry.
- Polished images: website headers, institutional campaigns, leadership features, and intentionally composed portraits.
- Favor warm natural light, natural expressions, environmental context, and natural skin tones.
- Keep text-safe areas clear and protect faces from overlays or responsive crops.
- Use blue overlays only when required for readable text.
- Use the supplied church family artwork only as a contextual legacy example; it already contains typography and graphics and must not be represented as a clean original photograph.
- Do not substitute unrelated stock photographs for absent CCBC images.

## COPY — RENDER EXACTLY

Use the numbered page copy from `brand-guide/content/ccbc-brand-guide-copy.md` without paraphrasing institutional language.

Locked text:

- Public name: **CCBC**
- Formal name: **Candelaria Conservative Baptist Church**
- Tagline: **Going for Christ, Growing in Christ**
- Brand pillars: **Welcoming, Biblical, Family-oriented**
- Personality: **Warm. Clear. Encouraging. Contemporary. Trustworthy.**

Do not paraphrase:

- The church name
- The tagline
- Hex values
- Logo rules
- The approved bilingual voice example
- The 18-page section titles

Where an application needs sample information, use only generic non-factual copy:

- Community Fellowship
- Date · Time · Location
- Learn more

Do not invent a real event, schedule, Scripture reference, contact detail, ministry, or church claim.

## LOGO HANDLING

- Place the supplied full logo without alteration.
- Maintain clear space equal to at least the height of one “C” in the CCBC wordmark on every side.
- Use the full-color logo master `ccbc-logo-typeset-high-resolution.png` only on White, Warm Cream, or another quiet light background.
- Do not fabricate reversed, monochrome, or wordmark-only versions. For a seal-only mark, use the approved `ccbc-seal-round-final.png`.
- Keep the tagline separate from the logo.
- The logo master is 3701 × 1083 px. Never enlarge it beyond acceptable visual quality; inspect the seal text at final output size.

## APPLICATIONS

### Bulletin

- Calm and structured.
- Comfortable reading size and predictable hierarchy.
- Include a White-background low-ink alternate view.
- Ensure essential distinctions survive grayscale.

### Website

- Spacious, welcoming, responsive, and action-oriented.
- One primary message and one primary action.
- Protect faces and essential details across crop ratios.

### Newsletter

- Editorial and story-led.
- Short sections with descriptive headings and clear scanning.
- Favor real ministry stories and specific invitations.

### Event poster

- One bold headline.
- One primary visual idea.
- Date, time, location, and response action grouped visibly.
- No decorative clutter or competing photographs.

## CONSTRAINTS

- FONT: Manrope + Inter only.
- STYLE: Clear Contemporary.
- MODE: Warm light with confident blue fields.
- CHANGE POLICY: During review, vary only one of crop, accent placement, or card arrangement at a time.
- Keep all text editable.
- Use native Canva shapes; do not flatten whole pages into screenshots.
- Preserve exact page count and order.
- Preserve A4 portrait dimensions.
- Make the final guide usable in full color, grayscale, and ordinary office printing.

## NEGATIVE PROMPT

- No logo reconstruction, recoloring, stretching, cropping, separation, rearrangement, or effects.
- No extra colors.
- No script fonts.
- No glass effects, neon, plastic HDR, 3D decoration, or decorative gradients.
- No generic megachurch stock imagery.
- No invented Scripture, ministry names, service times, contact details, or claims.
- No extra text beyond the approved copy.
- No text smaller than the specified print minimums.
- No multiple competing focal points.
- No gold or Clear Sky small text on White.
- No permanent tagline attachment to the logo.
- No sermon-slide system.
- No ministry sub-identities in this edition.
