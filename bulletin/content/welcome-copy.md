# Cover welcome copy

Twelve versions of the cover welcome, in English and Filipino, to rotate one per Sunday. Each sits under the heading "Welcome to our church family." on the cover.

Status: drafted by Claude on 2026-09-29. The Filipino has not been checked by a native speaker.

## How to use

1. Use one version each Sunday, in order, then start again.
2. Choose the English or the Filipino line.
3. In Canva, click the welcome text on the cover and paste it in.
4. Keep the heading and the "New here?" text as they are.

The same twelve versions are on page 4 of each Canva template.

## Fit

Every line is under 130 characters. That is two lines on the bifold cover and three to four on the trifold cover.

## Versions
