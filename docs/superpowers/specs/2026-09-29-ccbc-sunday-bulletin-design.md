# CCBC Sunday bulletin template: design

Date: 2026-09-29
Status: draft, awaiting review

## Purpose

Put brand guide version 1.1 to work on a real weekly piece. The result is a reusable Canva template that a volunteer fills in each week. The newsletter and the website follow as separate projects, each with its own spec.

## Decisions made

| Topic | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Contents | Order of service, announcements, and visitor welcome |
| Format | Bifold: one A4 sheet, landscape, printed both sides, folded once into four A5 panels |
| Direction | Calm only: Warm Cream ground, Deep Navy and CCBC Blue type, restrained Gold and Clear Sky accents |
| Cover | Cover B: theme artwork as a strip across the top, then logo, date, welcome, and tagline |
| Annual theme | "Walk with Jesus", from the church's "Banners" design |
| Theme artwork | The church's own artwork, recolored to the brand palette, with the schedule lines removed |
| Weekly editor | A volunteer working in Canva |
| Language | English headings. Filipino is welcome in any content slot |
| Content | Labeled placeholders. No invented names, dates, songs, or announcements |

## Real content

Only these items are real. Everything else is a placeholder.

- Theme: Walk with Jesus
- Tagline: Going for Christ, Growing in Christ
- Sunday Worship Service, 7:30 AM
- Wednesday Midweek Prayer, 11:00 AM
- Friday Prayer Meeting, 6:00 PM

## Canva design

- One new design, "CCBC Sunday Bulletin — Template", in the "CCBC Brand Guide" folder.
- Page size 1123 × 794 px, A4 landscape, the same scale as the brand guide master.
- Three pages: outside of the sheet, inside of the sheet, and a how-to page that is not printed.
- Each printed page holds two panels, each 561.5 px wide, with the fold on the center line. No fold line is printed.
- Panel margins are 40 px on every side, so text stays 40 px clear of the fold and of the sheet edge.

## Panels

### Page 1, outside of the sheet

**Right panel: cover**

1. Theme artwork strip across the full panel width, about 40% of the panel height, cropped from the vertical theme artwork.
2. Gold rule under the strip.
3. CCBC logo master on Warm Cream, with the clear space the guide requires.
4. Date placeholder: `[Sunday, date]`.
5. Heading: "Welcome to our church family."
6. Welcome text placeholder, two lines.
7. Gold rule and tagline at the foot.

**Left panel: back**

1. Heading: "New here?" with a placeholder paragraph.
2. Heading: "Join us" with the three real schedules.
3. Heading: "Giving" with a placeholder.
4. Heading: "Contact" with placeholders for address, phone, and social links.
5. Standalone seal at the foot.

### Page 2, inside of the sheet

**Left panel: order of service**

1. Heading: "Order of service".
2. Ten rows. Each row has an item label and a detail line.
3. The sermon row is highlighted with a Gold marker and holds the sermon title, the speaker, and the scripture reference.

**Right panel: announcements**

1. Heading: "Announcements".
2. Five blocks. Each has a title, a date line, and two lines of detail.
3. A verse panel at the foot on Deep Navy with White text: a verse placeholder and its reference.

Ten rows and five blocks replace the eight and four in the mockups, to fill the empty lower half of each inside panel.

### Page 3, how-to

Short numbered steps for the volunteer:

1. Make a copy of the template. Never edit the template itself.
2. Type over every bracketed placeholder. Delete any row that is not needed.
3. Check that no square brackets remain.
4. Download as PDF Print, pages 1 and 2 only.
5. Print double sided, flipped on the short edge, then fold with the cover outside.

## Type and color

| Use | Setting |
| --- | --- |
| Panel headings | Manrope bold, 24 px, Deep Navy |
| Subheadings and row labels | Inter bold, 14 px, CCBC Blue |
| Body and detail lines | Inter regular, 13 px, Charcoal |
| Date | Inter bold, 14 px, CCBC Blue |
| Tagline | Inter regular, 12 px, Charcoal |
| Verse panel | Inter regular, 14 px, White on Deep Navy |

Body text at 13 px prints at about 10 pt. No text is smaller than 12 px. Gold and Clear Sky are never used for small text.

## Placeholders

- Every placeholder is wrapped in square brackets and names what belongs there, such as `[Song title]`.
- One text box holds one slot, so the volunteer never reflows a layout.
- A leftover bracket is easy to spot before printing.

## Known limits

- **Fonts.** The Canva connection cannot choose a typeface. Text must be made by rewriting text that already carries Manrope or Inter, copied from the brand guide master. If a slot cannot be built that way, it is listed for the owner to set by hand from the Brand Kit.
- **Theme typeface.** The theme artwork keeps its original typeface unless the owner changes it by hand in the vertical trial design before the build.
- **Locking.** The connection cannot lock elements. The how-to page tells the volunteer to work on a copy.
- **Theme artwork does not follow the guide's photography rules** by the owner's choice. The rest of the bulletin follows the guide.
- **Photo sharpness** at print size has not been measured. The build measures it and reports the result.

## Checks before hand-off

1. Exported PDF has two A4 landscape pages.
2. Only Manrope and Inter appear in the PDF font list, apart from the theme artwork.
3. No text sits within 40 px of the fold or the sheet edge.
4. Every placeholder is bracketed, and the only real content is the list above.
5. A test print, double sided and folded, reads in the right order: cover, inside left, inside right, back.
6. The cover still reads clearly when printed in grayscale.

## Out of scope

- The low-ink version.
- The trifold layout.
- Filled-in weekly content.
- The newsletter and the website.
