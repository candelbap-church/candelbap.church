Logo misuse
The CCBC logo is a protected identifier. Keep its proportions, relationship, and clarity
intact across every application.


Never:
    Redraw, recolor, stretch, compress, crop, or rotate the logo.
    Separate or rearrange the seal and wordmark. Use the approved standalone seal file
    instead.
    Add outlines, shadows, glows, bevels, or other effects.
    Place the logo on a busy photograph.
    Put the current white-background image inside an improvised box.
    Attach the tagline permanently to the logo.
    Use a screenshot or social media copy as production artwork.

Graphic elements and layout
A simple system for clear movement and growth

Use a simple grid, generous margins, and strong left-aligned headlines. Let either one
message or one photograph lead the page.

Graphic elements

accents. Restrained angled bands, rising lines, and expanding blocks may suggest
movement and growth.

Voice and bilingual writing
Warm, clear, encouraging, biblical, and Christ-centered




     CCBC communicates with a warm, clear,
     encouraging, biblical, and Christ-centered voice.

Voice is the consistent way CCBC sounds across announcements, invitations, teaching, care, and
community updates. Keep one dominant message, make the next step clear, and leave room for
people to respond with hope.


Voice principles
    Personal — Address people directly and make the invitation feel human.
    Clear — Use familiar words, short sentences, and one clear next step.
    Hopeful — Invite with grace rather than guilt, pressure, or hype.
    Biblical — Keep the message grounded in Christ without relying on unexplained church
    jargon.


Voice
Address people personally. Use direct sentences, familiar words, and specific next steps. Invite
with hope rather than guilt, pressure, or hype. Avoid unexplained church jargon and academic
language.


Bilingual writing
Use English, Filipino, or a natural bilingual blend according to the audience and occasion. Do not
force both languages into every message. Translate for meaning and tone rather than mirroring
each word mechanically.
Let the audience, setting, and pastoral purpose guide the language choice. A natural blend should
feel welcoming and easy to understand.

Production standards and
pre-publication checklist
Ready for review, print, and publication

A calm final check for consistent, accessible, production-ready CCBC communications.


Production standards
Use these standards across print, digital, church-office, and professional production workflows.
    Use full-color and grayscale-safe layouts.
    Provide low-ink options for church-office printing.
    Use high-resolution, CMYK-ready assets for professional print.
    Include appropriate bleed and safe margins when required.
    Test every template at its intended output size.
    Keep editable source files with approved exports.
    Add useful alternative text to meaningful digital images.

Pre-publication checklist
Confirm each item before publishing, printing, or distributing.


     ☐ Is the approved CCBC logo intact, legible, and properly spaced?
     ☐ Are the church name and tagline written exactly?
     ☐ Are all dates, times, locations, and response actions correct?
     ☐ Are Scripture references accurate and is the translation identified?

     ☐ Is text readable with sufficient contrast?
