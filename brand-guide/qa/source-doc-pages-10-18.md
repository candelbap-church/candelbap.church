Voice & Bilingual Writing

CCBC voice

CCBC communicates with a warm, clear, encouraging, biblical, and Christ-centered voice.

Voice is the consistent way CCBC sounds across announcements, invitations, teaching, care, and community updates. Keep one dominant message, make the next step clear, and leave room for people to respond with hope.

Voice principles

- Personal — Address people directly and make the invitation feel human.
- Clear — Use familiar words, short sentences, and one clear next step.
- Hopeful — Invite with grace rather than guilt, pressure, or hype.
- Biblical — Keep the message grounded in Christ without relying on unexplained church jargon.

Address people personally. Use direct sentences, familiar words, and specific next steps. Invite with hope rather than guilt, pressure, or hype. Avoid unexplained church jargon and academic language.

Bilingual writing

Use English, Filipino, or a natural bilingual blend according to the audience and occasion. Do not force both languages into every message. Translate for meaning and tone rather than mirroring each word mechanically.

Focused bilingual callout

Let the audience, setting, and pastoral purpose guide the language choice. A natural blend should feel welcoming and easy to understand.

Production standards

Use these standards across print, digital, church-office, and professional production workflows.

- Use full-color and grayscale-safe layouts.
- Provide low-ink options for church-office printing.
- Use high-resolution, CMYK-ready assets for professional print.
- Include appropriate bleed and safe margins when required.
- Test every template at its intended output size.
- Keep editable source files with approved exports.
- Add useful alternative text to meaningful digital images.

Production flow · Prepare → Check → Export → Archive

01 · Prepare Approved assets and source files

02 · Check Content, contrast, permissions

03 · Export Correct preset and final size

04 · Archive Editable source and approved export

Pre-publication checklist

Confirm each item before publishing, printing, or distributing.

- Is the approved CCBC logo intact, legible, and properly spaced?
- Are the church name and tagline written exactly?
- Are all dates, times, locations, and response actions correct?
- Are Scripture references accurate and is the translation identified?
- Do all featured images have appropriate permission?
- Is text readable with sufficient contrast?
- Does the layout work in grayscale and at final size?
- Was the correct export preset used?
