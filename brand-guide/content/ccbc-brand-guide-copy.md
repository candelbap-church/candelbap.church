## 2. How to use this guide

This guide helps everyone who communicates on behalf of CCBC create materials that feel clear, consistent, and recognizably ours. Use it for bulletins, the website, newsletters, and event posters.

The Canva master is the editable source of truth. Use approved logo files, colors, typefaces, photography, and writing guidance. When a situation is not covered here, choose the clearest solution that remains welcoming, biblical, family-oriented, and Christ-centered.

**Public name:** CCBC  
**Formal name:** Candelaria Conservative Baptist Church  
**Personality:** Warm. Clear. Encouraging. Contemporary. Trustworthy.

## 5. Name and tagline

Lead with **CCBC** in everyday communication. Use **Candelaria Conservative Baptist Church** for formal identification, legal clarity, and first-use context for people who may not know us.

Our tagline is:

**Going for Christ, Growing in Christ**

The two parts express outward mission and inward formation. Use the tagline selectively on institutional materials. Keep it separate from the logo; do not attach it permanently to the artwork or force it onto every event asset.

## 6. Logo overview

Subtitle: Institutional mark standards

The existing CCBC logo is our primary institutional mark. Preserve it exactly and use the complete approved artwork.

Use the full-color logo on white, Warm Cream, or another quiet light background. Use only formally prepared official reversed or single-color artwork on dark or photographic backgrounds.

Do not recreate the logo from screenshots, social media images, or typed text.

### Logo and tagline

Keep the logo separate from the tagline. The tagline remains an independent text treatment and must not be locked into the logo artwork.

Use the approved logo artwork only. Keep clear space around the mark and preserve its proportions, color, and relationship to the page.

### Standalone seal

The seal may stand alone, without the wordmark, where the full logo cannot fit: profile images, favicons, stamps, and small badges. The full logo remains the primary mark.

Keep clear space of at least half the seal's width on every side. Use the approved seal file only, and never pair it with a retyped wordmark.

## 7. Logo clear space and minimum size

Subtitle: Technical standards for protecting the official CCBC mark across print and digital applications.

Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least the height of one **C** in the CCBC wordmark on every side. No text, photograph, border, or graphic element may enter this area.

### Minimum size

The approved master file is 3701 × 1083 pixels. Keep it at a size where the small text in the seal remains legible, and never enlarge it past acceptable visual quality. Final minimum sizes must be based on tests of the best available official master.

### Conservative size note

When in doubt, use the larger reproduction size. Confirm seal legibility before release and use the best available official master file.

## 8. Logo misuse

The CCBC logo is a protected identifier. Keep its proportions, relationship, and clarity intact across every application.

Never:

- Redraw, recolor, stretch, compress, crop, or rotate the logo.
- Separate or rearrange the seal and wordmark. Use the approved standalone seal file instead.
- Add outlines, shadows, glows, bevels, or other effects.
- Place the logo on a busy photograph.
- Put the current white-background image inside an improvised box.
- Attach the tagline permanently to the logo.
- Use a screenshot or social media copy as production artwork.

## 11. Typography

Subtitle: Manrope + Inter

A clear type system gives every CCBC message a confident voice. Use Manrope to lead attention and Inter to support reading, navigation, and everyday information.

### Manrope

Use Manrope for headings, display text, event titles, and major calls to action. Use Bold or ExtraBold for major headings and Medium or Semibold for subheadings.

### Inter

Use Inter for body text, schedules, labels, captions, newsletters, and website interface text. Use Regular for paragraphs and Semibold for functional labels.

### Hierarchy

| Role | Typeface and weight | Specimen |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Display | Manrope ExtraBold | One clear message |
| Heading | Manrope Bold | Typography that leads |
| Subheading | Manrope Semibold | Build a readable rhythm |
| Body | Inter Regular | Clear hierarchy keeps every message readable for older readers and first-time visitors alike. |
| Label | Inter Semibold | SECTION LABEL |
| Caption | Inter Regular | Caption text supports the main message. |
| CTA | Manrope Bold | Explore the guide |

Use Arial as a fallback only when brand fonts are unavailable. Prefer sentence case, left-aligned paragraphs, and clear hierarchy. Reserve widely spaced uppercase text for short labels. Do not use decorative script fonts or introduce additional typefaces.

## 13. Photography guidance

Subtitle: Real people. Real relationships. Real church life.

Photography should make the life of Candelaria Conservative Baptist Church feel visible, welcoming, and true. Show real people, real relationships, and real church life. Aim for approximately 60% candid and 40% polished photography.

### The 60 / 40 balance

**Candid, 60%.** Natural moments and active community life.

**Polished, 40%.** Intentional portraits and composed feature imagery.

### Choose the image for the moment

**Candid.** Use candid photography to show participation, connection, and everyday ministry in motion.

- Worship — shared attention, singing, prayer, and welcome.
- Fellowship — conversation, laughter, meals, and belonging.
- Service — people contributing with care and purpose.
- Discipleship — learning, mentoring, and growing together.
- Everyday ministry — the ordinary moments that build church life.

**Polished.** Use polished photography when a clear subject, composed frame, or confident presence supports the message.

- Website headers — establish a focused visual welcome.
- Major campaigns — carry a deliberate message across key touchpoints.
- Leadership features — introduce people with clarity and warmth.

### Editorial framing

Use open compositions, visible context, and generous negative space. Let the subject feel present in a real place rather than isolated from community.

- Candid frame. Crop cue: keep gestures, relationships, and environmental context visible.
- Polished frame. Crop cue: give portraits a calm, intentional focal point.

Reflect the actual people and community of Candelaria. Favor natural expressions, warm light, environmental context, and natural skin tones. Avoid generic stock worship scenes, exaggerated emotion, heavy filters, artificial lens effects, and staged ‘perfect family’ images.

### Permission

Obtain appropriate permission before prominently featuring children or identifiable people.

## 14. Graphic elements and layout

Subtitle: A simple system for clear movement and growth

Use a simple grid, generous margins, and strong left-aligned headlines. Let either one message or one photograph lead the page.

### Graphic elements

Use large blue fields for identity, Clear Sky shapes for energy, and Gold for focused accents. Restrained angled bands, rising lines, and expanding blocks may suggest movement and growth.

| Pattern | Layout | Best use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Statement | Blue field · left-aligned headline · one dominant message | Use a strong field and generous negative space to make one idea unmistakable. |
| Standards | Rising line · aligned columns · restrained Gold marker | Use a measured grid to present rules, principles, or repeatable guidance. |
| Application | Expanding block · information group · clear response | Use connected blocks to move the reader from context to action. |

### Grid and spacing

Six-column grid · 15 mm outer margins · 18 mm binding-side margin · 4 mm gutters · 8 pt spacing rhythm

| Step | Use |
| --- | --- |
| 8 pt | Small separation within a related group |
| 16 pt | Space between supporting elements |
| 24 pt | Space between content blocks |
| 32 pt | Major section transition |

Group date, time, location, and response action into one easy-to-scan information block. Keep logo placement consistent near a top or bottom edge with full clear space. Preserve the same typography, color roles, spacing rhythm, and photographic treatment as layouts change.

### Layout check

Keep one dominant message, align every element to the six-column grid, and preserve generous negative space. Use White and Warm Cream as breathing room; reserve Gold for focused accents.

## 15. Voice and bilingual writing

Subtitle: Warm, clear, encouraging, biblical, and Christ-centered

CCBC communicates with a warm, clear, encouraging, biblical, and Christ-centered voice.

Voice is the consistent way CCBC sounds across announcements, invitations, teaching, care, and community updates. Keep one dominant message, make the next step clear, and leave room for people to respond with hope.

### Voice principles

- Personal — Address people directly and make the invitation feel human.
- Clear — Use familiar words, short sentences, and one clear next step.
- Hopeful — Invite with grace rather than guilt, pressure, or hype.
- Biblical — Keep the message grounded in Christ without relying on unexplained church jargon.

Address people personally. Use direct sentences, familiar words, and specific next steps. Invite with hope rather than guilt, pressure, or hype. Avoid unexplained church jargon and academic language.

### Bilingual writing

Use English, Filipino, or a natural bilingual blend according to the audience and occasion. Do not force both languages into every message. Translate for meaning and tone rather than mirroring each word mechanically.

Let the audience, setting, and pastoral purpose guide the language choice. A natural blend should feel welcoming and easy to understand.

### Make the invitation human

Instead of:

“Attendees are hereby encouraged to participate in our forthcoming fellowship activity.”

Use:

“Come and spend time with your CCBC family. Sama-sama tayong magpalakasan at lumago kay Cristo.”

## 20. Production standards and pre-publication checklist

Subtitle: Ready for review, print, and publication

A calm final check for consistent, accessible, production-ready CCBC communications.

### Production standards

Use these standards across print, digital, church-office, and professional production workflows.

- Use full-color and grayscale-safe layouts.
- Provide low-ink options for church-office printing.
- Use high-resolution, CMYK-ready assets for professional print.
- Include appropriate bleed and safe margins when required.
- Test every template at its intended output size.
- Keep editable source files with approved exports.
- Add useful alternative text to meaningful digital images.

### Production flow

Prepare → Check → Export → Archive

1. Prepare. Approved assets and source files.
2. Check. Content, contrast, permissions.
3. Export. Correct preset and final size.
4. Archive. Editable source and approved export.

### Pre-publication checklist

Confirm each item before publishing, printing, or distributing.

- Is the approved CCBC logo intact, legible, and properly spaced?
- Are the church name and tagline written exactly?
- Are all dates, times, locations, and response actions correct?
- Are Scripture references accurate and is the translation identified?
- Do all featured images have appropriate permission?
- Is text readable with sufficient contrast?
- Does the layout work in grayscale and at final size?
- Was the correct export preset used?
