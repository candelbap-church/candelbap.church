# CCBC newsletter template: design

Date: 2026-09-29
Status: approved in conversation. Build in progress.

## Purpose

A quarterly newsletter for Candelaria Conservative Baptist Church, built on brand guide version 1.1. It is the second piece to put the guide to work, after the Sunday bulletin.

## Decisions made

| Topic | Decision |
| --- | --- |
| Name | Going and Growing, from the tagline |
| Frequency | Quarterly for now. First planned as monthly |
| Formats | Printed A4, PDF to share, and a social image set |
| Length | Two pages, one A4 sheet printed both sides |
| Sections | Lead story, pastor's note, ministry updates, calendar of events, prayer items, thanksgiving items |
| Ministries | Left as `[Ministry name]` placeholders |
| Paper | White. The page background is White, not Warm Cream |
| Language | English headings. Content in English or Filipino |
| Weekly editor | A volunteer working in Canva |

## Deliverables

| Order | Deliverable | Size |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | Newsletter template | A4 portrait, 794 × 1123 px, two pages |
| 2 | Social image set | 1080 × 1350 px, five images |

This spec covers the newsletter. The social image set follows once the newsletter is approved.

## Layout

Margins 56 px. Content width 682 px. Footer rule at top 1050, tagline at top 1065.

### Page 1

| Area | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Masthead | Logo left, "Going and Growing" right, quarter line beneath, Gold rule |
| Theme strip | "Walk with Jesus" artwork, 682 × 150 px |
| Lead story | Headline, photo frame 420 × 280 px, caption, body of about 150 words |
| Pastor's note | Warm Cream side panel with a Gold edge, heading, body of about 120 words, name |

### Page 2

| Area | Contents |
| --- | --- |
| Ministry updates | Three columns, each with a ministry name and a short update |
| Calendar of events | Six rows of date and event, and one poster frame |
| Prayer and thanksgiving | Two bulleted lists side by side, four items each |
| Contact panel | Deep Navy panel with the schedules, address, phone, email, four icons, and `candelbap.church` in White |

## Type

| Use | Setting |
| --- | --- |
| Newsletter name | Manrope bold, 30 px, Deep Navy |
| Section headings | Manrope bold, 22 px, Deep Navy |
| Lead headline | Manrope bold, 24 px, Deep Navy |
| Labels | Inter bold, 13 px, CCBC Blue |
| Body | Inter regular, 13 px, Charcoal |
| Caption | Inter regular, 11 px, Charcoal |

The caption at 11 px is the one text below the bulletin's 12 px floor. It prints at about 8 pt.

## Known limits

- The Canva connection cannot choose a typeface. Text boxes are rewritten copies of brand guide text.
- The theme strip shows the theme in Manrope. The bulletin covers still show the theme in its original typeface.
- The name has not been checked against other churches or publications.
- The social icons are recolored. Each platform's rules on recoloring have not been checked.

## Checks before hand-off

1. The exported PDF has two A4 portrait pages.
2. Only Manrope and Inter appear in the PDF font list.
3. Every placeholder is bracketed, and the real content matches the list above.
4. Nothing overlaps and nothing crosses the margins.
5. The pages read clearly in grayscale.
