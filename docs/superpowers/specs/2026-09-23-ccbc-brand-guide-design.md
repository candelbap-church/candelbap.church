# CCBC Brand Guide — Design Specification

**Date:** 2026-09-23  
**Church:** Candelaria Conservative Baptist Church  
**Public-facing name:** CCBC  
**Tagline:** Going for Christ, Growing in Christ

## 1. Purpose

Create a durable visual and verbal identity system for CCBC that can govern both digital and printed communications. The system must feel unmistakably CCBC, remain familiar to current members, and present the church warmly and clearly to young families in Candelaria.

The initial guide covers the general institutional identity. Ministry-specific identifiers such as CCBC Youth and CCBC Men will be designed later as an endorsed extension of the approved master system.

## 2. Audiences

1. Current CCBC members
2. Young families in Candelaria

The identity must retain continuity and trust for established members while remaining contemporary, approachable, and legible to families encountering CCBC for the first time.

## 3. Brand Foundation

### Brand pillars

- **Welcoming:** warm, approachable, relational, and open to newcomers
- **Biblical:** Scripture-centered, trustworthy, and grounded
- **Family-oriented:** multigenerational, communal, and supportive

### Positioning

CCBC is a welcoming, Scripture-centered church family where people go for Christ and grow together in Christ.

### Personality

- Warm
- Clear
- Encouraging
- Contemporary
- Trustworthy

### Tagline

**Going for Christ, Growing in Christ**

The two-part line expresses outward mission and inward formation. It should appear selectively on institutional materials and should not be permanently attached to the logo artwork or required on every event asset.

No permanent Scripture anchor is assigned in this first edition. Scripture selections must suit the specific context and use a clearly identified, faithful Bible translation.

## 4. Scope

### Included applications

- Church bulletins
- CCBC website
- Email and printable newsletters
- Event posters

### Deferred work

- Ministry identifiers and sub-brand rules
- Sermon slide system, which already exists separately
- Redesign or modification of the existing logo
- Campaign-specific identities

## 5. Identity Architecture

The existing CCBC logo remains the primary institutional mark and must be preserved exactly. The approved standalone seal is a secondary mark for places where the full logo cannot fit. Everyday communication leads with **CCBC**. The full name, **Candelaria Conservative Baptist Church**, appears where formal identification, legal clarity, or first-time audience context requires it.

The future ministry system will use an endorsed architecture: the unchanged CCBC identity plus standardized descriptors such as “CCBC Youth” and “CCBC Men.” Ministry marks may later receive controlled identifiers while remaining recognizably part of the master identity.

## 6. Visual Direction

The approved visual direction is **Clear Contemporary**:

- Bold, highly legible typography
- Clean modular layouts
- Confident blue fields
- Warm, restrained gold accents
- Fresh supporting blue
- Authentic, people-centered photography
- Simple geometric forms suggesting forward movement and growth

The system must avoid appearing corporate, cold, ornamental, or trend-dependent. Warm photography, generous spacing, and encouraging language provide the human balance to the structured contemporary style.

## 7. Color System

| Color | Hex | Primary role |
| --- | --- | --- |
| CCBC Blue | `#1E3A8A` | Primary brand fields, covers, headers, and emphasis |
| Deep Navy | `#172554` | Text, overlays, formal applications, and strong contrast |
| CCBC Gold | `#C8A64B` | Rules, highlights, icons, and restrained accents |
| Clear Sky | `#6DB7DD` | Fresh supporting panels, shapes, and event graphics |
| Warm Cream | `#FAF8F3` | Preferred warm light background |
| Charcoal | `#1F2937` | Primary body text |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Clarity, contrast, and low-ink layouts |

Recommended visual balance:

- 60% light neutral
- 25% blue or navy
- 10% Clear Sky
- 5% gold accent

Gold and Clear Sky must not be used for small text on white. Final guide production must document accessible foreground/background pairings and grayscale-safe alternatives.

## 8. Typography

### Primary families

- **Manrope:** headings, display text, event titles, and major calls to action
- **Inter:** body text, schedules, labels, captions, newsletters, and website interface text
- **Arial, sans-serif:** fallback when brand fonts are unavailable

### Principles

- Prefer sentence case over excessive capitals.
- Use Bold or ExtraBold Manrope for major headings.
- Use Medium or Semibold for subheadings and short emphasis.
- Use Regular Inter for paragraphs and Semibold for functional labels.
- Keep paragraphs left-aligned.
- Reserve spaced uppercase text for short category, ministry, or date labels.
- Establish hierarchy through size, weight, and spacing rather than extra typefaces.
- Do not use decorative script fonts.

## 9. Logo Policy

- Use the complete existing CCBC logo as the primary mark.
- Maintain clear space of at least the height of one “C” in the CCBC wordmark on all sides.
- Never redraw, recolor, stretch, compress, crop, rotate, outline, rearrange, or add effects.
- Never detach or rearrange the seal and wordmark. Where the seal must stand alone, use the approved standalone seal file, without the wordmark.
- Use the standalone seal for profile images, favicons, stamps, and small badges, with clear space of at least half its width on every side.
- Never pair the standalone seal with a retyped wordmark.
- Use the full-color logo on white, Warm Cream, or another quiet light background.
- Use only formally prepared reversed or single-color assets on dark or photographic backgrounds.
- Never create an improvised white box around the logo.
- Keep the tagline separate from the logo artwork.
- Use approved master files rather than screenshots or copies taken from social media.

The current available logo appears to be a small raster file. Before final technical rules are published, production must locate the highest-resolution or vector master. Minimum sizes must be determined from legibility tests of the actual approved asset, particularly the small text in the seal.

## 10. Photography and Imagery

Use a balanced mix of approximately 60% candid and 40% polished photography.

### Candid photography

- Worship, fellowship, service, discipleship, and everyday ministry
- Natural expressions and genuine interaction
- Multigenerational participation and family relationships

### Polished photography

- Website headers
- Major institutional campaigns
- Leadership features
- Intentionally composed portraits or group photographs

### General standards

- Reflect the actual people and community of Candelaria.
- Preserve natural skin tones.
- Favor warm natural light and enough environmental context to tell a story.
- Avoid generic stock worship imagery, exaggerated emotion, heavy filters, artificial lens effects, and staged “perfect family” scenes.
- Use blue overlays only when required for readable text.
- Keep geometric graphics from obscuring faces or important action.
- Obtain appropriate permission before publicly featuring children or identifiable individuals.

## 11. Layout System

The system is modular rather than template-locked.

- Build on a simple grid with generous margins and spacing.
- Use strong, primarily left-aligned headlines.
- Let either the message or one photograph lead the composition.
- Use large blue fields for brand recognition, Clear Sky shapes for energy, and gold for focused accents.
- Use restrained geometric forms such as angled bands, rising lines, and expanding blocks to suggest movement and growth.
- Group date, time, place, and response action into one scannable information block.
- Keep logo placement consistent near a top or bottom edge with required clear space.
- Preserve the same typography, color roles, spacing rhythm, and photographic treatment even when individual layouts vary.

### Application behavior

- **Bulletins:** calm, structured, readable, and economical to print
- **Website:** spacious, welcoming, responsive, and action-oriented
- **Newsletters:** editorial, story-led, and easy to scan
- **Event posters:** one dominant headline, one primary image or graphic idea, and immediately visible event details

## 12. Voice and Language

CCBC communicates with a warm, clear, and encouraging voice.

- Address people personally and with genuine care.
- Use direct sentences, familiar words, and specific next steps.
- Invite participation with hope rather than guilt, pressure, or hype.
- Remain biblical without relying on unexplained church jargon or academic language.
- Keep messages Christ-centered rather than institution-centered.
- Use English, Filipino, or a natural bilingual blend according to the audience and occasion.
- Do not force both languages into every message.
- Translate for meaning and tone rather than mechanically mirroring each word.

Example transformation:

> Formal: “Attendees are hereby encouraged to participate in our forthcoming fellowship activity.”
>
> CCBC voice: “Come and spend time with your CCBC family. Sama-sama tayong magpalakasan at lumago kay Cristo.”

## 13. Accessibility and Production

- Maintain accessible text contrast.
- Keep bulletin and newsletter body text comfortable for older readers.
- Do not place essential text over visually busy image areas.
- Never encode meaning through color alone.
- Add useful alternative text to meaningful website and newsletter images.
- Provide full-color and grayscale-safe template behavior.
- Provide low-ink versions for ordinary church-office printing.
- Prepare CMYK-ready, high-resolution assets for professional print use.
- Include appropriate bleed and safe margins in professional print exports.
- Keep editable source files alongside approved distribution exports.

Every production template must be tested at its intended output size rather than approved only at screen-preview scale.

## 14. Deliverable and Workflow

The editable **Canva design is the source of truth**. The production sequence is:

1. Build the complete brand guide in Canva.
2. Review typography, layout, colors, logo handling, and sample applications.
3. Revise and approve the Canva master.
4. Export the approved guide as a polished, print-ready PDF.

The PDF is the primary distribution document. The Canva master remains the editable source for future maintenance.

The guide should include:

1. Cover and introduction
2. Brand foundation and pillars
3. Name and tagline usage
4. Logo standards and misuse examples
5. Color palette and approved combinations
6. Typography and hierarchy examples
7. Photography and imagery direction
8. Graphic elements and layout principles
9. Voice and bilingual writing guidance
10. Application examples for the four in-scope channels
11. Accessibility, print, and export standards
12. Pre-publication checklist

## 15. Acceptance Criteria

The guide is ready for approval when:

- The existing logo has not been altered.
- Every page visibly belongs to the Clear Contemporary direction.
- The palette and type system are applied consistently.
- Examples demonstrate use across bulletins, website, newsletters, and event posters.
- Photography feels authentic to CCBC and Candelaria.
- The writing sounds warm, clear, encouraging, and naturally bilingual where appropriate.
- Core guidance remains usable in full color, grayscale, professional print, and ordinary office print.
- The Canva master is fully editable and organized for future maintenance.
- The exported PDF renders cleanly with no clipping, substitution, low-resolution imagery, or broken links.
- The pre-publication checklist covers logo integrity, spelling, event details, Scripture references, image permission, contrast, and export settings.
