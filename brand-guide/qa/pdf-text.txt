Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least the height of one C in the CCBC
wordmark on every side. No text, photograph, border, or graphic element may enter this
area.

Logo misuse
Never:
    Redraw, recolor, stretch, compress, crop, or rotate the logo.
    Separate or rearrange the seal and wordmark. Use the approved standalone seal file
    instead.
    Add outlines, shadows, glows, bevels, or other effects.
    Place the logo on a busy photograph.
    Put the current white-background image inside an improvised box.
    Attach the tagline permanently to the logo.
    Use a screenshot or social media copy as production artwork.

Graphic elements and layout
Use a simple grid, generous margins, and strong left-aligned headlines. Let either one
message or one photograph lead the page.




Angled band                            Rising line                             Expanding blocks


Use large blue fields for identity, Clear Sky shapes for energy, and Gold for focused
accents. Restrained angled bands, rising lines, and expanding blocks may suggest
movement and growth.


Grid and spacing




Six-column grid · 15 mm outer margins · 18 mm binding-side margin · 4 mm gutters · 8 pt spacing rhythm



Group date, time, location, and response action into one easy-to-scan information block.
Keep logo placement consistent near a top or bottom edge with full clear space.
Preserve the same typography, color roles, spacing rhythm, and photographic treatment
as layouts change.

Voice and bilingual writing

     CCBC communicates with a warm, clear,
     encouraging, biblical, and Christ-centered voice.


Voice
Address people personally. Use direct sentences, familiar words, and specific next
steps. Invite with hope rather than guilt, pressure, or hype. Avoid unexplained church
jargon and academic language.


Bilingual writing
Use English, Filipino, or a natural bilingual blend according to the audience and occasion.
Do not force both languages into every message. Translate for meaning and tone rather
than mirroring each word mechanically.

Production standards and
pre-publication checklist
Production standards
    Use full-color and grayscale-safe layouts.
    Provide low-ink options for church-office printing.
    Use high-resolution, CMYK-ready assets for professional print.
    Include appropriate bleed and safe margins when required.
    Test every template at its intended output size.
    Keep editable source files with approved exports.
    Add useful alternative text to meaningful digital images.




Before publishing

     ☐ Is the approved CCBC logo intact, legible, and properly spaced?
     ☐ Are the church name and tagline written exactly?
     ☐ Are all dates, times, locations, and response actions correct?
     ☐ Are Scripture references accurate and is the translation identified?
     ☐ Do all featured images have appropriate permission?
     ☐ Is text readable with sufficient contrast?
     ☐ Does the layout work in grayscale and at final size?
     ☐ Was the correct export preset used?
