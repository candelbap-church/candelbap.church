# CCBC volunteer guide: copy

The wording of the volunteer guide, as built in Canva on 2026-09-29. The Canva design "CCBC Volunteer Guide" is the copy volunteers read. Change both together.

Canva menu names are written from memory of the app. Check them against the live Canva screens.

## Page 1. Volunteer guide

How to prepare the Sunday bulletin and the quarterly newsletter in Canva.

You do not need design experience. The templates already carry the church's colors, fonts, and logo. Your part is to put in the right words and pictures, check them, and print.

### Three rules

1. Work on a copy. Never change a template.
2. Type over the square brackets. Leave nothing in brackets.
3. Check before you print: dates, times, names, and spelling.

### Who to ask

Jeth looks after the Canva account. [How to reach Jeth]

In this guide: how the account is organized, the weekly bulletin, the quarterly newsletter, brand rules in brief, the check before you print, and bringing in a new volunteer.

## Page 2. How the account is organized

Templates are in Brand Templates. Everything else is in the folder CCBC Brand Guide.

### Folders

- Brand Templates, in the Brand section of Canva. The bulletin, trifold, and newsletter templates. Opening one gives you a fresh copy.
- 2 Bulletins — weekly issues. One bulletin for each Sunday. Your weekly work goes here.
- 3 Newsletters — quarterly issues. One newsletter for each quarter.
- 4 Artwork and posters. Theme artwork and event posters. Put new posters here.
- Archive — do not edit. Old files. Leave it alone.
- CCBC Brand Guide — Master. The full brand guide. Read it. Do not edit it.

### Naming your files

- Bulletin: Bulletin 2026-10-04
- Newsletter: Newsletter 2026 Q4
- Put the year first, so the files sort in order.

### Who can change what

- Account owner: members, the Brand Kit, and the templates.
- Volunteer: make copies, edit your own copies, download, and print.

## Page 3. The Sunday bulletin

Each week. About 30 minutes.

### Before you start, collect

- The order of service, with song titles.
- The sermon title, the speaker, and the scripture reference.
- The announcements, each with its date and time.
- Up to two event posters, if any.
- The verse of the week, with its reference.

### Steps

1. In Canva, open Brand, then Brand Templates. Choose the bifold or the trifold bulletin.
2. Select Use this template. Canva opens a fresh copy for you.
3. Rename the copy, for example Bulletin 2026-10-04. Move it to the folder 2 Bulletins.
4. Cover: type the date. Paste this week's welcome from page 4 of the template.
5. Order of service: type over each row. Delete rows you do not need.
6. Announcements: type over each block. Delete blocks you do not need.
7. Trifold only: drag each poster onto a poster frame. Delete a frame you do not need.
8. Verse: type the verse and its reference.
9. Check the whole sheet, using the checklist in this guide.
10. Select Share, then Download, then PDF Print. Choose pages 1 and 2 only.
11. Print one copy, double sided, flipped on the short edge. Fold it and read it.
12. If the test copy is right, print the rest.

### Folding

- Bifold: fold once, with the cover on the outside.
- Trifold: fold the right panel in first, then the left panel over it.

## Page 4. The newsletter

Each quarter. About two hours, once the stories are written.

### Before you start, collect

- The lead story, about 150 words, and one photograph.
- A caption for the photograph, and permission from the people in it.
- The pastor's note, about 120 words.
- Three ministry updates, three or four lines each.
- The events for the quarter, with dates and times, and one poster if any.
- Prayer items and thanksgiving items, up to four of each.

### Steps

1. In Canva, open Brand, then Brand Templates. Choose the newsletter.
2. Select Use this template. Canva opens a fresh copy for you.
3. Rename the copy, for example Newsletter 2026 Q4. Move it to the folder 3 Newsletters.
4. Masthead: type the quarter and year. Type the same in the footer of both pages.
5. Lead story: type the headline and the story. Drag the photograph onto the photo frame. Type the caption.
6. Pastor's note: paste the note and type the pastor's name.
7. Ministry updates: type the ministry names and the updates.
8. Calendar: type each date and event. Drag the poster onto the poster frame.
9. Prayer and thanksgiving: type the items.
10. Check both pages, using the checklist in this guide.
11. For print: Share, then Download, then PDF Print. Print double sided, flipped on the long edge.
12. For sharing online: Share, then Download, then PDF Standard.

## Page 5. Brand rules in brief

The full rules are in CCBC Brand Guide — Master.

### Colors

- CCBC Blue · #1E3A8A · labels and details
- Deep Navy · #172554 · headings and panels
- Gold · #C8A64B · thin rules and one accent
- Clear Sky · #6DB7DD · supporting accent
- Warm Cream · #FAF8F3 · quiet panels
- Charcoal · #1F2937 · body text
- White · #FFFFFF · page background

### Fonts and logo

Manrope for headings. Inter for everything else. Both are in the Brand Kit. Use the logo from the Brand Kit, keep clear space around it, and place it on white or another quiet, light background.

### Do

- Keep text in the boxes the template gives you.
- Use real photographs of our own church family, with permission.
- Keep one main message on a page.

### Do not

- Stretch, recolor, or retype the logo.
- Use Gold or Clear Sky for small text.
- Add new fonts, new colors, or gradients.
- Shrink text to make more fit. Cut words instead.

## Page 6. Check before you print

Go through every line, every time.

### The checklist

- No square brackets remain anywhere.
- The date on the cover is this Sunday's date.
- Every date, time, and place is correct.
- Names are spelled correctly.
- Scripture references are correct.
- No text is cut off or overlapping.
- Every photograph has permission from the people in it.
- Only pages 1 and 2 are in the PDF.
- One test copy was printed and folded before the full run.
- A second person has read it.

### If something looks wrong

Stop and ask before you print the full run. A short delay costs less than a reprint.

## Page 7. Bringing in a new volunteer

A first session of about one hour.

### For the account owner

1. Invite the person to the Canva team, under Settings, then People.
2. Give them the volunteer role.
3. Share the folder CCBC Brand Guide with them.
4. Send them this guide.

### For the new volunteer

1. Read this guide from start to finish.
2. Open CCBC Brand Guide — Master and look through it.
3. Open the bulletin Brand Template and make a practice copy. Name it Practice, then your name.
4. Fill it in with last Sunday's details.
5. Download it, print one copy, and fold it.
6. Show it to the person who trained you.
7. Delete the practice copy when you are done.

First two real bulletins: prepare them with an experienced volunteer beside you. From the third, work alone, and have a second person read it before printing.

When a volunteer steps down: the account owner removes them under Settings, then People. Their bulletins stay in the folders.
